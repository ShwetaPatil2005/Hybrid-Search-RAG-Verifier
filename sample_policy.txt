Company Leave & Work Policy 2026

1. Working Hours & Attendance
Standard office hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM IST, Monday through Friday. All employees are expected to log their daily hours in the internal portal.

2. Sick Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 12 paid sick leaves per calendar year. Any continuous sick leave exceeding 3 business days requires a mandatory medical certificate signed by a registered healthcare practitioner. Failure to provide a certificate will result in unpaid leave for the extended duration.

3. Remote Work & WFH Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior approval from their direct manager. Remote work requests must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance via the HR dashboard.

4. Equipment & Internet Allowance
Full-time remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office setup stipend of $500 and a monthly internet allowance of $50. Receipts must be uploaded within 30 days of purchase for reimbursement.
